## 🚨 The Global Problem (Initial focus: Europe)
Most business owners cannot successfully sell or transition their companies.

- 75% exit failure rate — 70–80% of attempted sales do not complete.  
- 150,000 viable businesses lost annually in Europe due to failed transitions.  
- 600,000 jobs disappear every year from failed handovers in Europe.  

In the U.S., 4.5M businesses worth $10–14T must transition in the next decade, with no structured infrastructure.

---

## ❌ Why Transfers Fail (Root Cause Analysis)
The ecosystem is broken at multiple critical points.

### Owner Challenges
- No succession plan  
- Unclear valuation  
- No access to qualified buyers  
- Emotional attachment

### Buyer Obstacles
- Fragmented deal flow  
- Poor data  
- Wasted time  
- No trusted verification  

### Outcomes
- Slow processes  
- Stressful negotiations  
- Failed transitions  
- Value destruction

AnyBiz creates a structured, guided AI-driven workflow — from first contact to money in the bank.

#### 01 — Smart Intake Bot

#### 02 — AI Dealroom & Automated Outreach
- One centralized workspace

#### 03 — White-Glove Human Concierge
- Expert guide joins after due diligence  
- Secure, aligned communication  
- Drives deal to a successful exit  
- Supports ongoing cooperation
